Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025

Link:
https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/

Main findings:

This source was used to understand how developers currently feel about AI tools in software development. The survey shows that AI tools are now common in programming, but developers still have concerns about trust and accuracy.

The most important finding for this assignment is that AI-generated answers still need human checking. Even if AI can explain code, generate examples, or suggest fixes, developers cannot assume that the answer is correct every time.

Connection to my topic:

This connects directly to human skills in the AI era because it shows that critical thinking and verification are still necessary. If developers do not check AI output, they may introduce bugs, security problems, or incorrect logic into a project.

How I used it:

I used this source to support the idea that software engineers will still need judgment, technical understanding, and responsibility even when AI tools are available.

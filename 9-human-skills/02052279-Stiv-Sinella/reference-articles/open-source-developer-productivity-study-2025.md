Open-source Developer Productivity Study 2025

Link:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089

Main findings:

This research paper studied how early-2025 AI tools affected experienced open-source developers. The study involved 16 developers completing 246 tasks in mature projects where they already had experience.

The main finding was surprising. Developers expected AI to make them faster, but the study found that allowing AI tools actually increased task completion time by about 19%. This means AI slowed the developers down in this specific setting.

Important idea:

AI does not automatically improve productivity in every software engineering situation. In complex or familiar codebases, experienced developers may spend extra time checking, correcting, or adapting AI suggestions.

Connection to my topic:

This connects directly to human skills because it shows why judgment, verification, experience, and systems thinking are still important. If AI output is close but not exactly correct, the developer still needs to understand the project deeply enough to fix it.

How I used it:

I used this source to support the point that AI should be treated as an assistant, not as a replacement for software engineering skill. Human developers are still responsible for checking quality, understanding context, and making final decisions.

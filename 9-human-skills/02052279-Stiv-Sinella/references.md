References

1. Stack Overflow. (2025). Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025.
Link: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/

Used for:
This source was used to understand how developers currently view AI tools, especially trust, accuracy, and the need for human verification of AI-generated answers.

2. Google Cloud DORA. (2025). State of AI-assisted Software Development 2025.
Link: https://dora.dev/dora-report-2025/

Used for:
This report was used to support the idea that AI is becoming part of software development workflows, but teams still need strong engineering practices, human judgment, testing, collaboration, and clear responsibility.

3. McKinsey & Company. (2025). Superagency in the workplace: Empowering people to unlock AI’s full potential at work.
Link: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full-potential-at-work

Used for:
This report was used to discuss the economic potential of generative AI in work environments, including software engineering, and why developers need to adapt instead of ignoring AI.

4. GitHub. (2025). Octoverse 2025: The state of open source.
Link: https://octoverse.github.com/

Used for:
This source was used to understand how AI, agents, and modern development tools are changing open-source software development and developer workflows.

5. GitHub Blog. (2025). Octoverse: A new developer joins GitHub every second as AI leads TypeScript to #1.
Link: https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-a-new-developer-joins-github-every-second-as-ai-leads-typescript-to-1/

Used for:
This article was used as supporting evidence that AI is becoming a major part of modern software development and that developers are adapting their tools and languages.

6. Paradis, E., Grey, K., Madison, Q., Nam, D., Macvean, A., Meimand, V., Zhang, N., & Ferrari-Church, B. (2024). How much does AI impact development speed? An enterprise-based randomized controlled trial.
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12944

Used for:
This research paper was used to support the point that AI can help developers finish some tasks faster, but its effect depends on the task, developer experience, and development environment.

7. Becker, J., Rush, N., Barnes, E., & Rein, D. (2025). Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity.
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089

Used for:
This paper was used to show that AI does not always improve productivity automatically. In some real open-source tasks, AI tools made experienced developers slower, which supports the need for human judgment and careful verification.
